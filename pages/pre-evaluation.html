Your evaluation, step by step

  1. 01

    Check in.

    Come to the front desk in Suite 280.

  2. 02

    Talk.

    Dr. Chenthitta asks about the pain and what it's stopping you from doing.

  3. 03

    Exam and imaging.

    He examines you and goes over any imaging with you. If you need imaging, he orders it.

  4. 04

    Your diagnosis.

    He explains where the pain is coming from, what else it could be and why other treatments haven't worked yet, in plain language.

  5. 05

    His recommendation.

    He recommends what fits you, and why. If PRP isn't right for you, he says so and explains what he'd do instead.

  6. 06

    Cost.

    If you want to go ahead, you get the full cost first.

How to get ready for your visit

What to think about:

  • What you can't do today that you want to get back to.
  • How long you've had the pain, and what makes it worse.
  • Past treatments, what did or didn't help and what other doctors have told you.

What to bring:

  • Any MRI or x-ray reports, and a list of the medications you take. No imaging? That's fine.
  • Your questions, written down.
  • Whoever helps you decide, if you'd like them there.

Please arrive a few minutes early, in loose clothes that make the sore joint easy to examine.

Cost and common questions

Will insurance cover my treatment?

No. PRP and MLS laser treatments are self-pay, and insurance doesn't cover them.

What will it cost?

It depends on the joint and what Dr. Chenthitta finds. He goes over the full cost with you at your evaluation, before you commit to anything.

Does it hurt?

Dr. Chenthitta talks you through each step. One patient wrote that they were "terrified of needles... it was not painful at all." Individual results vary.

How long do results last?

It depends on the joint and how much damage there is, so Dr. Chenthitta won't promise a number. He tells you what's realistic once he's examined you, and he checks in with you at three months.

What if I'm not a candidate?

Dr. Chenthitta tells you why, and what he'd do instead.
