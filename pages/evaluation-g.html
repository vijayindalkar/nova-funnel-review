What the next 90 days can look like.

Joint and back pain doesn't always start where you feel it. It can come from a tendon, a nearby joint or your spine.

At your evaluation, Dr. Chenthitta finds which one is causing your pain. Then he treats that spot.

The treatment is placed right where the pain starts, using image guidance. Then you come back for laser sessions, here in the office.

The goal is to support your body's own repair, with no incision and no hospital stay.

  • Get out of the car without bracing on the door.
  • Play the Saturday game, then stay for coffee.
  • Sleep on either side through the night.
  • Get down on the floor with the grandkids, and back up.

Questions people ask before they book

Is this treatment safe?

As with any injection, there are small risks such as soreness or bruising, which Dr. Chenthitta reviews with you.

What does it cost, and does insurance cover any of it?

These treatments are self-pay, and insurance doesn't cover them. There isn't one price, because your treatment depends on the joint and what Dr. Chenthitta finds. He goes over the full cost with you at your evaluation, before you commit to anything.

Does it hurt?

Dr. Chenthitta talks you through each step. One patient wrote that they were "terrified of needles... it was not painful at all." Individual results vary.

How long do results last?

It depends on the joint and how much damage there is, so Dr. Chenthitta won't promise a number. He tells you what's realistic once he's examined you, and he checks in with you at three months.

Do I need an MRI or x-ray first?

No. If you have one, bring it. If you need imaging, Dr. Chenthitta orders it.

What if I'm not a candidate?

Dr. Chenthitta tells you at your evaluation and explains what he'd do instead.
