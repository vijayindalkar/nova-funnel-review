Pain doesn't always start where you feel it, so at your evaluation, Dr. Chenthitta checks the tendons, nearby joints and spine. Treatment may be PRP (platelet-rich plasma), made from a small sample of your blood and placed where the pain starts, using image guidance. Laser sessions follow. Questions people ask before they book

Is this treatment safe?

Because PRP is made from your own blood, allergic reaction and rejection are very unlikely. As with any injection, there are small risks such as soreness or bruising, which Dr. Chenthitta reviews with you.

What does it cost, and does insurance cover any of it?

PRP and laser treatments are self-pay, and insurance doesn't cover them. There isn't one price, because your treatment depends on the joint and what Dr. Chenthitta finds. He goes over the full cost with you at your evaluation, before you commit to anything.

Does it hurt?

Dr. Chenthitta explains each step as he goes, and what you'll feel. One patient wrote: "I am terrified of needles... While it was a tad uncomfortable it was not painful at all." Individual results vary.

How long do results last?

It depends on the joint and how much damage there is, so Dr. Chenthitta won't promise a number. He tells you what's realistic once he's examined you, and he keeps checking in with you as your treatment goes on.

Do I need an MRI or x-ray first?

No. If you have one, bring it. If you need imaging, Dr. Chenthitta orders it.

What if I'm not a candidate?

Dr. Chenthitta tells you at your evaluation and explains what he'd do instead.
